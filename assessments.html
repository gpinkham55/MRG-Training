Section 7: Assessments

Week 1 Quiz: P21 Navigation, Customers, Items, and Industrial Hose

Time: 60 minutes | Pass: 8 of 10 written + both scenarios correct

Written questions

  1. Before entering an order for a mill account, which three things do you check on the customer record first? a) Salesrep, fax number, website b) Credit status, notepads, PO requirements c) Year opened, number of ship-tos, company logo d) Last invoice amount only

  2. A customer gives you their own part number "CAPC-77812." What P21 feature turns that into our Item ID? a) Item Search by description b) Customer Part Number cross-reference c) Pricing library d) Notepad

  3. A customer opens a new delivery site in another county. You should: a) Overwrite their main ship-to address b) Create a new ship-to with the correct tax group for the new address c) Type the new address in the order notes only d) Use the bill-to address and tell the driver

  4. A ship-to can be set tax exempt when: a) The customer says they're exempt b) The buyer emails that they're exempt c) A valid exemption certificate is on file with accounting d) The order is over $1,000

  5. A gasket sheet item sells by the square inch (SI). A customer wants one 10" x 10" piece. What quantity do you enter? a) 1 b) 10 c) 100 d) 3,600

  6. In STAMPED, the "M" stands for: a) Manufacturer b) Material / Media c) Measurement d) Maximum pressure

  7. Item Inquiry shows 254 ft on hand and 50 ft allocated at location 110. How many feet can you promise a new customer? a) 254 b) 204 c) 50 d) 304

  8. Which tube compound should never be used for petroleum oil? a) Nitrile (NBR) b) EPDM c) FKM d) UHMWPE

  9. A cam and groove Type C is: a) Adapter × female NPT b) Coupler × hose shank c) Adapter × hose shank d) Coupler × male NPT

  10. Which attachment method is correct for a steam hose assembly? a) Two preformed bands b) Worm gear clamps c) Ground joint fittings with interlocking clamps d) Cam and groove Type E with bands

Practical P21 scenarios

Scenario 1W-A (counter or phone): A practice caller from Alexander Sealcoating & Striping (10364): "I need 2 of the 3-inch aluminum Type C cam and groove couplers and 20 more feet of the same 3-inch suction hose we got back in April. Use PO A15200." Perform in P21PLAY: customer lookup, confirm ship-to, check notepads, find the April hose in history, check available stock on both items, and enter the order with the right units, PO, carrier, and freight.

Scenario 1W-B: A practice caller from Montgomery Water Works (10606): "I'm Rachel Kim, the new AP specialist. Invoices should come to me at rkim@practice-example.com." Perform in P21PLAY: search for duplicates, look at the contacts already on the account, add the new contact with the AP role, decide what to do with the existing invoice contact, set invoice delivery, and add notes.

Week 2 Quiz: Hydraulics, Crimping Standards, Quoting

Time: 75 minutes | Pass: 8 of 10 written + both scenarios correct

Written questions

  1. A -12 hydraulic hose has an inside diameter of: a) 1/2" b) 3/4" c) 1-1/4" d) 12 mm

  2. Which hose has a constant 4,000 psi working pressure from -6 through -16? a) 100R1 b) 100R2 c) 100R12 d) 100R17

  3. Compared to wire braid hose, spiral hose generally has: a) Lower pressure ratings and tighter bend radius b) Higher pressure and impulse ratings, larger bend radius c) The same ratings at lower cost d) No wire reinforcement

  4. A -8 JIC male thread measures: a) 1/2-20 b) 9/16-18 c) 3/4-16 d) 13/16-16

  5. A fitting with an O-ring in a groove on the nose face is: a) JIC b) ORFS c) NPT d) SAE O-ring boss

  6. Crimp target 0.975", tolerance ±0.005". You measure 0.968". What do you do? a) Ship it, close enough b) Re-crimp at a tighter setting c) Scrap the end; it is over-crimped d) Recrimp at a looser setting

  7. OAL is 30". Fitting A cutoff allowance 1.10", fitting B 1.60". Cut length is: a) 27.30" b) 28.40" c) 31.10" d) 32.70"

  8. Typical proof test pressure for a hydraulic assembly is: a) Equal to working pressure b) 2× working pressure c) 4× working pressure (burst) d) 10× working pressure

  9. A quote costs $84.00 and sells at $120.00. The gross margin is: a) 30% b) 42.9% c) 36% d) 70%

  10. A customer accepts a quote. The correct P21 action is: a) Enter a new order and leave the quote open b) Convert the quote to an order and enter the PO c) Email the quote PDF to shipping d) Delete the quote

Practical crimp / fitting identification scenarios

Scenario 2W-A (bench): You receive a fitting with a male thread measuring 0.74" OD, 16 threads per inch, parallel threads, and a 37° cone on the nose. A second fitting has a male thread measuring 0.81" OD, 16 TPI, parallel, with an O-ring groove on the end face of the nose. Identify both. Then build a 24" OAL -8 100R2 assembly connecting a female swivel to each. List the fittings needed, look up C/O values, calculate cut length, and crimp, measure, and record.

Scenario 2W-B (troubleshooting): A customer's 100R2 -8 assembly blew a fitting off after two days. The returned assembly shows: insertion mark 3/8" back from the ferrule edge, crimp diameter 0.020" over target, the hose is Brand X, the fittings are Brand Y. Identify every cause and state what MR&G does differently.

Final Comprehensive Exam: P21, Hose & Fittings, Sealing & Gaskets

Time: 90 minutes | Pass: 85% (13 of 15)

  1. A long-time mill customer calls for an urgent order. P21 shows Credit Status: Hold. What do you do? a) Enter and release it; they're a good customer b) Enter the order, explain the hold politely, and route to accounting for release c) Refuse to take the order d) Enter it under a cash account

  2. A customer says "send it to our new pump house, same as the plant tax." The pump house is in a different county. You: a) Use the plant ship-to's tax group b) Create a new ship-to and assign the tax group for the pump house address c) Mark it exempt d) Leave tax off and let accounting fix it

  3. You're asked to quote a hose assembly. The customer gives size, pressure, and ends only. Which STAMPED elements are still missing? a) None b) Temperature, Application, Material/Media, Delivery c) Only Delivery d) Only Temperature

  4. Correct suction hose for pulling water from a sump: a) Collapsible discharge hose b) Hose with a rigid helix wire, rated for vacuum c) 100R1 hydraulic hose d) Any 150 psi water hose

  5. A fitting has a 60° thread form, tapers, and seals on the threads. It is: a) BSPP b) JIC c) NPT d) ORFS

  6. Which pair is easiest to confuse with calipers alone? a) -4 JIC and -16 JIC b) 1/2" NPT and 1/2" BSPP c) ORFS and SAE flange d) Cam and groove A and C

  7. A system runs 3,000 psi with frequent pressure spikes to 3,800 psi on a -16 line. Which hose is the best fit? a) 100R1 -16 b) 100R2 -16 c) 100R12 -16 d) 100R17 -16

  8. Why must hose and fittings come from the same manufacturer and series? a) Looks better b) Retention and crimp specs are only tested and approved for matched components c) P21 requires it d) It's cheaper

  9. You find a pinhole leak spraying from a hydraulic hose. How do you check it? a) Run your gloved hand along the hose b) Use cardboard and depressurize when possible; never use hands c) Squeeze the hose near the leak d) Wrap tape around it and recheck under pressure

  10. A 4" Class 150 RF ring gasket in 1/16" CNA has what OD and ID? a) 6.88" OD × 4.50" ID b) 9.00" OD × 4.50" ID c) 7.12" OD × 4.50" ID d) 6.88" OD × 4.00" ID

  11. The correct gasket for a cast iron FF pump flange is: a) Ring gasket, 1/16" graphite b) Full-face gasket with bolt holes c) Two stacked ring gaskets d) Spiral wound

  12. Max operating conditions are 200 psig at 300°F. What is the P×T value, and what else must you check? a) 500; nothing else b) 60,000; also confirm pressure and temperature each fall within the material's individual limits for that thickness c) 1.5; flange color d) 60,000; nothing else

  13. The correct star sequence for an 8-bolt flange (bolts numbered clockwise) is: a) 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 b) 1, 5, 3, 7, 2, 6, 4, 8 c) 1, 8, 2, 7, 3, 6, 4, 5 d) 1, 3, 5, 7, 2, 4, 6, 8

  14. On a 60" × 60" sheet with 0.125" spacing, how many 2" Class 150 ring gaskets (OD 4.12") fit using the square grid method? a) 100 b) 144 c) 196 d) 225

  15. Which gasket choice is best for chlorine dioxide bleach plant service on an FRP flange? a) Nitrile rubber b) Red SBR rubber c) Expanded PTFE, full face d) CNA ring, 1/8"
