SECTION 3: Daily Practical Exercises & Simulations

Each exercise is done in P21PLAY during the self-taught half, then reviewed with your mentor in Block D. Mentor initials each completed exercise.

Exercise 1.1 (Day 1): Navigation Scavenger Hunt

Find and record:

  1. The Customer ID for a mill account your mentor gives you by city only.
  2. That customer's terms and credit status.
  3. The last three orders: dates, PO numbers, and order totals.
  4. How many ship-tos the account has.
  5. One mandatory notepad on any account (mentor will point you to one).
  6. Look up item IDs for: a 1-1/2" cam and groove Type C aluminum, a -8 R2 hydraulic hose, and 1/16" CNA sheet.

Exercise 1.2 (Day 2): Read the Customer

Mentor assigns a pulp and paper account. Write a one-paragraph account brief: credit terms, typical items bought, average order size, PO requirements, key contacts, assigned salesrep, and anything in notepads a new rep must know. Then answer: "The customer calls asking for 'the same suction hose as last time.' What exactly did they buy, at what price, and when?"

Exercise 1.3 (Day 3): The New Buyer

Scenario: "Hi, this is Tanya Morales. I'm the new buyer at Central Alabama Paper Converters. Kevin Blake retired. I need to be on all your quotes and order confirmations. Invoices should still go to accounts payable at ap@capc-example.com." Tasks:

  1. Search for duplicates for Tanya and the AP email.
  2. Add Tanya as a Purchasing contact with phone and email.
  3. Set quote and acknowledgment delivery to Tanya.
  4. Confirm AP contact receives invoices.
  5. Mark Kevin Blake inactive and move his delivery roles.
  6. Add dated notes on both records. Role play: run a STAMPED interview with your mentor playing Tanya asking for "a hose for our pump."

Exercise 1.4 (Day 4): New Site Ship-To

Scenario: "We're opening a temporary job trailer at our Wetumpka site, 4100 River Rd, Gate B. Deliveries only during posted receiving hours, call Marcus at 334-555-0147 before arriving. Same tax as our main plant." Tasks:

  1. Search current ship-tos. Confirm it is new.
  2. Create the ship-to with Gate B in Address 2.
  3. Put the receiving-hours limit and call-ahead in delivery instructions, not address lines.
  4. Challenge the "same tax" assumption: look up the correct tax group for the Wetumpka address (Elmore County) and confirm it with your mentor.
  5. Set carrier to MR&G truck and freight code per account. Product question: The customer also wants 2" water discharge hose with cam and groove ends to fit a pump with a 2" male groove adapter. Which cam and groove type goes on the hose? (Answer: Type C coupler × hose shank.)

Exercise 1.5 (Day 5): Three Stock Orders

Enter, review with mentor, and release (P21PLAY):

  1. A walk-in counter order: 2 ea 1" Type C cam and groove, 50 ft 1" water hose, 4 bands. Cash customer.
  2. A phone order for a mill account requiring a PO and release number.
  3. An order where one line has only half the quantity available (set disposition to B, tell the "customer").

Exercise 2.1 (Day 6): Pricing, Tax, Freight

  1. Enter an order where the customer quotes a price 12% lower than P21's price. Find out whether a quote or contract supports it. If not, calculate margin at the requested price and decide whether to request approval.
  2. A customer says they are tax exempt for this order only. What do you need, and what do you do if it isn't on file?
  3. Enter one order Prepaid & Add via UPS, one Collect on the customer's FedEx account, one Will Call.
  4. Hydraulic: Customer needs a 3/4" hose for a 3,500 psi system on an excavator boom cylinder with constant cycling. Recommend a hose type and explain (Expected answer: -12 spiral, 100R12 (4,000 psi constant) for high impulse; R2 -12 is rated lower and braid has lower impulse life).

Exercise 2.2 (Day 7): Mystery Fittings

Identify 12 fittings from the tray. For each, record: OD measured, threads per inch, seat angle or seal type, and final ID (for example, "-8 JIC male, 3/4-16, 37°"). Then quote three of them in P21PLAY.

Exercise 2.3 (Day 8): Quote to Order

  1. Create a quote for: 10 ea 4" Class 150 RF ring gaskets, 1/16" CNA; 4 ea -8 R2 hose assemblies 48" OAL, JIC female swivel both ends. Set a 30-day expiration.
  2. Calculate margin on each line. Identify any line below MR&G's floor.
  3. Customer accepts 2 weeks later with PO 88412. Convert the quote to an order.
  4. Confirm the quote shows closed/converted.
  5. Run your open quote list and write a follow-up plan for any quote older than 14 days.

Exercise 2.4 (Day 9): Build and Verify

With Eric Jones present, build:

  1. -8 100R2, 36" OAL, female JIC swivel × female JIC swivel 90°. Calculate cut length from the catalog C/O values. Measure and record crimp diameters.
  2. -12 100R12, 24" OAL, ORFS female swivel both ends.
  3. Proof test assembly 1 at 2× working pressure. Record results. Complete the inspection checklist for each assembly.

Simulation 2.5 (Day 10): Quote, Convert, Assemble

Scenario: "Our hydraulic press at the Prattville shop blew a hose. It's a 1/2-inch two-wire hose, about 40 inches long, one end screws into a port and the other is a swivel. Can you get me a price and have it today?" Tasks:

  1. Run STAMPED. Discover: 3,000 psi, hydraulic oil, 150°F, port end is a male fitting going into an SAE O-ring boss port (-8), other end female JIC swivel.
  2. Quote the assembly with a 7-day expiration.
  3. Convert to an order on acceptance.
  4. Route the assembly to the shop and confirm a realistic ready time with Eric.
  5. Tell the customer: hose type, ends, length, price, ready time.

Exercise 3.1 (Day 11): Material Match

For each service, pick a gasket material and thickness and explain in one sentence:

  1. 3" Class 150 RF, 60 psig, 180°F caustic (white liquor)
  2. 6" Class 150 RF, 150 psig saturated steam (366°F)
  3. 4" FF cast iron water pump flange, 80 psig ambient water
  4. 2" Class 300 RF, 400 psig, 600°F hot oil
  5. 3" FRP flange, chlorine dioxide service, low pressure

Expected answers: (1) EPDM 1/8" or ePTFE 1/16"; (2) CNA rated for steam at 1/16" after P×T check, or graphite with insert; (3) full-face neoprene or SBR 1/8"; (4) flexible graphite with 316 insert (CNA out of range; consult); (5) ePTFE full-face, soft enough for FRP.

Exercise 3.2 (Day 12): Yield and Price a Gasket Run

Scenario: "I need 40 pieces 4-inch 150 pound raised face gaskets and 20 pieces 2-inch, same material, 1/16 blue CNA."

  1. Determine OD and ID for both sizes from Reference Sheet H.
  2. Calculate yield of 4" rings per sheet (use the actual sheet size in P21).
  3. Show how many 2" rings come from the 4" ring centers.
  4. Calculate material cost per piece and quote both lines at MR&G's target margin.
  5. Shadow Kevin as he nests the job and compare his yield to yours.

Exercise 3.3 (Day 13): Leak Call

Scenario: "Your gasket leaked on our 6-inch steam line. We put it in yesterday." (Class 150, 150 psig steam, 1/16" CNA ring.)

  1. Walk through the troubleshooting questions from Reference Sheet J.
  2. Discover: installer reused the old bolts dry, tightened in a circle in one pass, and used anti-seize on the gasket face.
  3. Write the reply to the customer: professional, helpful, no blame, with correct installation steps and the 8-bolt star sequence.
  4. Enter a no-charge replacement order only if your mentor approves.

Simulation 3.4 (Day 14): Six-Call Desk Simulation

Mentor plays callers in sequence. Trainee handles each start to finish in P21.

  1. Call 1: A maintenance planner from a known mill account needs "a 2-inch suction hose assembly, 20 feet, with a female cam and groove on one end and a male NPT on the other, delivered to our new wastewater building on Hwy 82." Trainee must: run STAMPED (media: wastewater with grit, ambient, suction from a sump, full vacuum), select a suction hose with rigid helix, select a Type C coupler on one end and a male NPT hose shank (combination nipple) on the other, choose attachment method (crimped ferrule or interlocking clamps), create the new ship-to with correct tax group, add the planner as a Maintenance contact if new, quote or enter per the customer's direction.
  2. Call 2: A buyer needs a quote on 100 ea 3" Class 300 RF ring gaskets, 1/16" ePTFE, quote valid 60 days. Trainee must flag that 60 days exceeds standard validity and confirm with mentor.
  3. Call 3: Walk-in brings a hydraulic fitting: male, 14 TPI, OD 0.83", threads parallel, 30° cone on the nose. Trainee identifies it (1/2" BSPP male, cone seat) and finds a replacement.
  4. Call 4: A customer on credit hold wants an order shipped today. Trainee enters the order, explains the hold politely, and routes to accounting.
  5. Call 5: Customer's AP clerk says invoices go to a retired employee. Trainee fixes contacts and delivery.
  6. Call 6: Customer accepts a quote from three weeks ago. Trainee converts it, enters the PO, and checks for expired pricing.

Scored on: accuracy, safety, customer communication, and data clean-up.
