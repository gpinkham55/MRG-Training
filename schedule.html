How to Use This Plan

This plan takes a new hire from first login to running an order desk on their own by Week 4. Each day pairs one Prophet 21 (P21) skill with one technical product skill, then puts both to use in shadowing and practice.

Training team

Role Mentor Primary topics
Program owner Greg, General Manager Orientation, assessments, Day 15 sign-off
Inside sales mentors Dominique Hitt, April Cleary Customer lookup, order entry, quoting, mill accounts
Counter and hose assembly lead Eric Jones Counter sales, hose and fitting ID, crimping, proof testing
Warehouse and waterjet Kevin Johnson Receiving, stock locations, gasket cutting, sheet yield

Ground rules for the trainee

  1. Wear safety glasses any time you cut hose or sheet, or operate shop equipment.
  2. The first half of each day is self-taught. Work through the assigned guide in P21PLAY and the assigned reference sheet on your own, and write down questions for your mentor.
  3. In Weeks 1 and 2, every live order or quote you key gets a mentor review before it is saved or released.
  4. Ask early. A two-minute question beats a wrong part on a truck to a paper mill.
  5. Keep a training notebook. Write down every new term, part number family, and P21 shortcut.

P21PLAY. P21PLAY is MR&G's P21 test system. All practice orders, quotes, ship-tos, and contacts go in P21PLAY, never in live P21, until your mentor moves you to the live system.

A note on P21 field labels. Field and tab labels below follow standard Prophet 21. MR&G's window layouts, security roles, and custom fields may differ slightly. On Day 1, the trainer walks each window live and the trainee marks any differences in this guide.

Daily structure (all days)

Half Block Length Content
First half (self-taught) Block A 2.0 hrs P21 self-study: work the assigned guide step by step in P21PLAY
First half (self-taught) Block B 2.0 hrs Technical self-study: reference sheets, catalogs, parts on the sample table
Second half (with mentor) Block C 2.5 to 3.0 hrs Shadowing and mentorship (phones, counter, shop)
Second half (with mentor) Block D 0.5 to 1.0 hr Daily practical exercise, questions from your notebook, recap

Each Block D opens with the trainee's questions from the self-taught half. The mentor checks the P21PLAY records the trainee created that day.

SECTION 1: 3-Week Training Master Schedule

Week 1: Foundations (Customers, Contacts, Ship-Tos, Basic Orders, Industrial Hose)

Day 1: Welcome and P21 Navigation

  • Focus: Company orientation, facility tour, P21 login and screen navigation.
  • Block A (P21): Greg or a mentor gets the trainee logged in to P21 and P21PLAY with a personal taker ID (not a shared counter login), then the trainee works on their own: tour the main menu, favorites, the search/lookup button, Tab-to-search behavior, and how to open multiple windows. Open Customer Master Inquiry then look up five accounts by ID, by partial customer name, and by phone number.
  • Block B (Technical): MR&G history (est. 1973, part of the TRG family), markets served (pulp and paper, chemical, municipal, general industrial), and product families: industrial hose, hydraulic hose, fittings and adapters, gaskets and sheet goods, packing, belting.
  • Block C (Shadow, 2.5 hrs): Facility tour with Kevin Johnson (receiving, stock locations, hose reels, sheet rack, waterjet). Equipment walk-through with Eric Jones (crimper, cutoff saw, test bench); safety glasses required when cutting or operating equipment. Observe the counter for 1 hour.
  • Block D: Exercise 1.1. Recap with Greg (30 min).

Day 2: Customer Lookup Deep Dive and Hose Construction

  • Focus: Reading a customer record end to end.
  • Block A (P21): Guide 2.1 (Customer Lookup). Credit limit, credit status, terms, order history, open orders, notepads, pricing library assignments, customer part numbers.
  • Block B (Technical): Hose anatomy: tube, reinforcement, cover. Hands-on with cut samples: water discharge, air, suction (helix wire), steam, chemical (UHMWPE), petroleum.
  • Block C (Shadow, 3 hrs): Sit with Dominique Hitt on the phones. Note every time she checks credit, history, or notes before answering.
  • Block D: Exercise 1.2.

Day 3: Contact Maintenance and the STAMPED Method

  • Focus: Clean contact records and the hose selection interview.
  • Block A (P21): Guide 2.5 (Contact Maintenance). Add a contact, link to a customer, set role tags (AP, Purchasing, Maintenance), phone, email, and document delivery.
  • Block B (Technical): Reference Sheet A (STAMPED). Practice the seven questions on each other with role play.
  • Block C (Shadow, 3 hrs): April Cleary on phones. Trainee listens for which STAMPED questions get asked, and which get skipped.
  • Block D: Exercise 1.3.

Day 4: Ship-To Maintenance and Industrial Fittings

  • Focus: Correct delivery and tax setup; industrial coupling types and attachment.
  • Block A (P21): Guide 2.2 (Ship-To Maintenance). Add a ship-to, assign tax group, salesrep/territory, carrier, freight code, delivery instructions.
  • Block B (Technical): Reference Sheet B (Industrial Fittings & Attachment). Cam and groove types A through F, combination nipples, ground joint steam fittings, interlocking clamps, bands, crimped ferrules. Material compatibility basics (Reference Sheet C).
  • Block C (Shadow, 2.5 hrs): Eric Jones at the counter and assembly bench. Watch a banded water assembly and a steam assembly with interlocking clamps get built.
  • Block D: Exercise 1.4.

Day 5: Order Entry Basics and Week 1 Quiz

  • Focus: A clean standard stock order from start to release.
  • Block A (P21): Guide 2.3 (Order Entry), steps 1 through 12. Enter three practice stock orders in P21PLAY on your own; mentor reviews them in Block D.
  • Block B (Technical): Self-review of Week 1 reference sheets (60 min), then Week 1 Quiz (60 min, taken at the start of the second half).
  • Block C (Shadow, 2 hrs): Trainee enters two live counter orders with Eric reviewing before release.
  • Block D: Exercise 1.5. Quiz review with Greg.
  • Pass standard: 80% on written questions; both P21 scenarios completed with no errors that would ship wrong product, bill wrong price, or bill wrong tax.

Week 2: Hydraulics, Crimping, and Quoting

Day 6: Advanced Order Entry and Hydraulic Hose Types

  • Focus: Backorders, cross-references, pricing and tax overrides, freight, release.
  • Block A (P21): Guide 2.3, steps 13 through 22 (dispositions, customer part numbers, price overrides, tax, freight, credit hold and release).
  • Block B (Technical): Reference Sheet D (Hydraulic Hose). SAE 100R1, R2, R12, R13, R15, R16, R17. Braid vs spiral. Working pressure, burst, impulse life, bend radius.
  • Block C (Shadow, 3 hrs): Dominique Hitt: watch two backorder situations and how the customer gets told.
  • Block D: Exercise 2.1.

Day 7: Quoting Part 1 and Thread Identification

  • Focus: Building a clear, accurate quote; identifying hydraulic threads by measurement.
  • Block A (P21): Guide 2.4 (Quoting), steps 1 through 10. Create three quotes: stock items, a non-stock item, and a hose assembly.
  • Block B (Technical): Reference Sheet E (Thread ID). Hands-on with calipers, thread pitch gauge, and seat angle gauge on a tray of 12 mystery fittings (NPT, JIC, ORFS, BSPP, BSPT, SAE O-ring boss, SAE flange).
  • Block C (Shadow, 2.5 hrs): Eric Jones: identify fittings brought in by walk-in customers.
  • Block D: Exercise 2.2.

Day 8: Quoting Part 2 and Crimp Spec Charts

  • Focus: Converting, tracking, and checking margin on quotes; reading crimp charts.
  • Block A (P21): Guide 2.4, steps 11 through 18 (expiration dates, converting a quote to an order, open quote follow-up, margin review).
  • Block B (Technical): Reference Sheet F (Crimp Spec Verification), parts 1 and 2. Reading the chart, die selection, crimper settings.
  • Block C (Shadow, 3 hrs): Dominique Hitt (MR&G's highest-volume quoter): open quote follow-up calls.
  • Block D: Exercise 2.3.

Day 9: Hose Assembly Orders and Hands-On Crimping

  • Focus: Building, measuring, testing, and documenting an assembly.
  • Block A (P21): Entering hose assembly orders (Guide 2.3, step 23), and how assemblies route to the shop.
  • Block B (Technical): Reference Sheet F parts 3 through 6. Trainee reads parts 3 through 6 on their own, then builds three assemblies with Eric in the second half: one 100R2 -8 with JIC ends, one 100R12 -12 with ORFS ends, one industrial crimped assembly. Measures crimp diameter, checks insertion depth, proof tests one.
  • Block C (Shadow, 2.5 hrs): Assembly bench with Eric.
  • Block D: Exercise 2.4. Eric decides when the trainee can crimp without him at the bench.

Day 10: Week 2 Quiz and Combined Simulation

  • Focus: Prove Week 2 skills.
  • Block A (P21): Simulation 2.5 (quote, convert, assembly order).
  • Block B: Week 2 Quiz (75 min), self-proctored.
  • Block C (Shadow, 2 hrs): Trainee takes phone calls with mentor on a split headset.
  • Block D: Quiz review with Greg.
  • Pass standard: 80% written; both crimp/fitting ID scenarios correct.

Week 3: Gaskets, Sealing, and Independent Operation

Day 11: Gasket Materials

  • Focus: Soft gasket material families and how to pick one.
  • Block A (P21): Gasket and sheet items in P21: sheet stock items, cut gasket items, customer part numbers for repeat gaskets, quoting cut-to-size parts.
  • Block B (Technical): Reference Sheet G (Gasket Material Selection). Elastomers, compressed non-asbestos (CNA), expanded PTFE, filled PTFE, flexible graphite. Sample board hands-on.
  • Block C (Shadow, 3 hrs): April Cleary on mill account calls (pulp and paper sealing questions).
  • Block D: Exercise 3.1.

Day 12: Flanges, Pressure Classes, and Sheet Yield

  • Focus: Sizing a ring or full-face gasket and pricing it from sheet.
  • Block A (P21): Quote a cut gasket run using sheet cost and yield (Guide 2.4, margin section).
  • Block B (Technical): Reference Sheet H (Flanges & Pressure Classes) and Reference Sheet I (Sheet Yield).
  • Block C (Shadow, 3 hrs): Kevin Johnson at the waterjet: how a gasket program gets nested and cut, kerf, scrap.
  • Block D: Exercise 3.2.

Day 13: Bolt Torque, P×T Limits, and Troubleshooting

  • Focus: Installation guidance and when to escalate.
  • Block A (P21): Review day: open orders, open quotes, backorder follow-up, contact clean-up on one assigned account.
  • Block B (Technical): Reference Sheet J (Bolt Torque Patterns & Installation). P×T calculations. Troubleshooting a leaking flange.
  • Block C (Shadow, 2.5 hrs): Trainee runs the phone with mentor listening; mentor only steps in when asked.
  • Block D: Exercise 3.3.

Day 14: Full Desk Simulation

  • Focus: Independent operation with a mentor close by.
  • Block A and B combined (4 hrs): Simulation 3.4 (six-call desk simulation). This is the one first half run with a mentor, who plays the callers.
  • Block C (3 hrs): Live desk. Trainee handles calls and counter orders; mentor reviews before release.
  • Block D: Final exam prep with Greg.

Day 15: Final Exam and Sign-Off

  • Block A and B: Final Comprehensive Exam (90 min). Review with Greg.
  • Block C: Live desk, reviews reduced to spot checks.
  • Block D: Week 4 plan: account assignments, check-in schedule (weekly for 30 days), and any remediation topics.
  • Pass standard: 85% on the final; all sign-offs complete (P21 Order Entry, P21 Quoting, Eric's OK to crimp solo).
