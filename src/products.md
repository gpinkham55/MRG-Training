# Section 4: Products & Capabilities

## Who We Are

Montgomery Rubber & Gasket has served central Alabama's pulp, paper, and industrial markets since 1973. We're part of the TRG family of companies, which means we can pull stock from other TRG locations when we're short. In P21, Montgomery is **location 110**. We're at 1201 Newell Pkwy, Montgomery, AL 36110.

Customers come to us three ways: they call, they email a PO, or they walk up to the counter. Every one of them wants the right part, on time, without having to explain it twice.

## What We Make and Do

### Hose assemblies

We build hydraulic and industrial hose assemblies to spec in our shop: cut, crimp, test, tag. Customers can also bring in a failed hose for us to copy, or a good hose for us to **cut and re-crimp** with new ends. In P21, an assembly is its own item with the hose and fittings on the lines beneath it.

What we need to build one: the seven STAMPED answers (Reference Sheet A).

### Waterjet cutting

Our waterjet cuts gaskets from sheet (ring, full face, and special shapes), and custom parts from rubber, plastic, insulation, and metal. We cut from our stock or from material the customer supplies. Waterjet time is billed as labor on the order.

What we need: a drawing, a sample, or the flange size and class, plus material and thickness.

### Custom fabrication

We make repeat parts to drawing for manufacturing customers: special gaskets, insulation panels, spacers, and similar parts. These usually have their own item IDs, often with the customer's part number in the description.

### Emergency and after-hours service

Mills and plants break down at all hours. We offer after-hours and weekend call-out service. Those calls carry an after-hours fee in P21. Your mentor will explain who's on call and how a call-out gets handled.

### Delivery and pickup

We deliver on our own truck, ship by carrier, or hold orders for will call. Ship-to delivery instructions (Guide 3.3) tell the driver what to do.

### TRG network

When we're short, another TRG location may have stock to transfer (disposition T in Guide 3.6). Always check before you backorder.
