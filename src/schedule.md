# Section 2: Three-Week Schedule

Each day follows the Daily Structure in Section 1: self-taught P21 and product study in the first half, mentor time in the second. Guide numbers refer to Section 3; reference sheet letters refer to Section 5.

## Week 1: P21 Basics, Customers, and Industrial Hose

### Day 1: Welcome and P21 Navigation

- **Focus:** Orientation, facility tour, logging in, and finding your way around P21.
- **Block A (P21):** Your mentor gets you logged in to P21 and P21PLAY with your own user ID. Then work Guide 3.1 (Navigation) on your own.
- **Block B (Product):** Read Section 4 (Products & Capabilities) start to finish. Walk the sample table and match each product line to a sample.
- **Block C (2.5 hrs):** Facility tour with the Shop Mentor: receiving, stock locations, hose reels, sheet rack, waterjet, crimpers, test bench. Safety glasses on in the shop. Observe the counter for 1 hour.
- **Block D:** Exercise 1.1. Recap with the Manager.

### Day 2: Customer Inquiry and Hose Construction

- **Focus:** Reading a customer record end to end.
- **Block A (P21):** Guide 3.2 (Customer Inquiry & Sales History).
- **Block B (Product):** Reference Sheets A (STAMPED) and B (Hose Sizing). Handle cut samples: water, air, suction, steam, chemical, petroleum.
- **Block C (3 hrs):** Shadow the Sales Mentor on phones and counter. Note every time they check credit, history, or notes before answering.
- **Block D:** Exercise 1.2.

### Day 3: Contact Maintenance and STAMPED

- **Focus:** Clean contact records and the hose selection interview.
- **Block A (P21):** Guide 3.4 (Contact Maintenance).
- **Block B (Product):** Practice the STAMPED questions out loud. Read Reference Sheet F (Hose Compatibility).
- **Block C (3 hrs):** Counter or phones with the Sales Mentor. Listen for which STAMPED questions get asked and which get skipped.
- **Block D:** Exercise 1.3.

### Day 4: Ship-To Maintenance and Connection Types

- **Focus:** Correct delivery and tax setup; the common connection types.
- **Block A (P21):** Guide 3.3 (Ship-To Maintenance).
- **Block B (Product):** Reference Sheets C (Pipe Sizes), D (Connection Types), and E (Industrial Fittings & Attachment).
- **Block C (2.5 hrs):** Shop Mentor at the counter and assembly bench. Watch a banded water assembly and a steam assembly get built.
- **Block D:** Exercise 1.4.

### Day 5: Item Inquiry and Week 1 Quiz

- **Focus:** Finding an item, its stock, its price, and where else it's stocked.
- **Block A (P21):** Guide 3.5 (Item Inquiry) and Reference Sheet O (Our P21 Item IDs & Units).
- **Block B:** Self-review of the week (60 min), then the **Week 1 Quiz** (60 min, at the start of the second half).
- **Block C (2 hrs):** Enter two live counter orders with the Shop Mentor or Sales Mentor reviewing before release.
- **Block D:** Exercise 1.5. Quiz review with the Manager.
- **Pass standard:** 80% on written questions; both P21 scenarios done with no errors that would ship wrong product, bill wrong price, or bill wrong tax.

## Week 2: Orders, Hydraulics, and Assemblies

### Day 6: Order Entry Basics and Hydraulic Hose

- **Focus:** A clean stock order from start to release.
- **Block A (P21):** Guide 3.6 (Order Entry), steps 1 through 12.
- **Block B (Product):** Reference Sheet G (Hydraulic Hose).
- **Block C (3 hrs):** Sales Mentor: watch orders get entered from phone and counter.
- **Block D:** Exercise 2.1.

### Day 7: Order Entry Advanced and Thread Identification

- **Focus:** Backorders, cross-references, price and tax, freight, release.
- **Block A (P21):** Guide 3.6, steps 13 through 23.
- **Block B (Product):** Reference Sheet H (Thread Identification). Hands-on with calipers, pitch gauge, and seat gauge on the mystery fitting tray.
- **Block C (2.5 hrs):** Shop Mentor at the counter: identify fittings walk-in customers bring in.
- **Block D:** Exercise 2.2.

### Day 8: Order Inquiry, Follow-Up, and Crimp Specs

- **Focus:** Answering "where's my order?" and reading crimp charts.
- **Block A (P21):** Guide 3.8 (Order Inquiry & Follow-Up).
- **Block B (Product):** Reference Sheet I (Assembly & Crimping), parts 1 and 2.
- **Block C (3 hrs):** Sales Mentor: backorder and order status calls.
- **Block D:** Exercise 2.3.

### Day 9: Hose Assembly Orders and Hands-On Crimping

- **Focus:** Building, measuring, testing, and documenting an assembly.
- **Block A (P21):** Guide 3.6, step 24 (hose assemblies). Look up three past assembly items in history and read how they were built.
- **Block B (Product):** Reference Sheet I, parts 3 through 8. Then build three assemblies with the Shop Mentor.
- **Block C (2.5 hrs):** Assembly bench with the Shop Mentor.
- **Block D:** Exercise 2.4. The Shop Mentor decides when you can crimp without them at the bench.

### Day 10: Quoting and Week 2 Quiz

- **Focus:** A clear, accurate quote, and converting it to an order.
- **Block A (P21):** Guide 3.7 (Quoting). Then Simulation 2.5.
- **Block B:** **Week 2 Quiz** (75 min).
- **Block C (2 hrs):** Take requests (phone or counter) with a mentor beside you.
- **Block D:** Quiz review with the Manager.
- **Pass standard:** 80% written; both crimp and fitting ID scenarios correct.

## Week 3: Gaskets, Our Capabilities, and Working on Your Own

### Day 11: Gasket Materials

- **Focus:** Soft gasket materials and how we sell sheet and cut gaskets in P21.
- **Block A (P21):** Look up our sheet items and cut gasket items (Reference Sheet O). Practice quoting a cut gasket.
- **Block B (Product):** Reference Sheet J (Gasket Materials).
- **Block C (3 hrs):** Sales Mentor on mill account requests.
- **Block D:** Exercise 3.1.

### Day 12: Flanges, Sheet Yield, and the Waterjet

- **Focus:** Sizing a ring or full-face gasket and pricing it from sheet.
- **Block A (P21):** Quote a cut gasket run using sheet and waterjet labor.
- **Block B (Product):** Reference Sheets K (Flanges) and L (Sheet Yield).
- **Block C (3 hrs):** Shop Mentor at the waterjet: nesting, kerf, scrap, and custom parts.
- **Block D:** Exercise 3.2.

### Day 13: Other Product Lines and Installation Help

- **Focus:** Belting, expansion joints, metal hose, O-rings, seals, packing; helping a customer with a leak.
- **Block A (P21):** Review day: open orders, open quotes, backorder follow-up, and contact clean-up on one assigned account.
- **Block B (Product):** Reference Sheets M (Bolt Torque & Installation) and N (O-Rings). Re-read the product lines in Section 4.
- **Block C (2.5 hrs):** You take requests; your mentor listens and steps in only when asked.
- **Block D:** Exercise 3.3.

### Day 14: Full Simulation

- **Focus:** Working on your own with a mentor close by.
- **Blocks A and B (4 hrs):** Simulation 3.4. This is the one first half run with a mentor, who plays the customers.
- **Block C (3 hrs):** Live phones or counter. Mentor reviews before release.
- **Block D:** Final exam prep with the Manager.

### Day 15: Final Exam and Sign-Off

- **Blocks A and B:** **Final Exam** (90 min). Review with the Manager.
- **Block C:** Live phones or counter; reviews drop to spot checks.
- **Block D:** Plan for Week 4: account or area assignments, weekly check-ins for 30 days, and any topics to review.
- **Pass standard:** 85% on the final; all sign-offs complete (P21 Order Entry, P21 Quoting, Shop Mentor's OK to crimp solo if you'll build assemblies).

## Mentor Hours

| Mentor | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sales Mentor | 7.0 | 8.0 | 6.0 | 21.0 |
| Shop Mentor | 6.5 | 7.0 | 4.0 | 17.5 |
| Manager | 2.5 | 1.5 | 3.0 | 7.0 |

Counter, warehouse, and shop hires shift about 4 hours from the Sales Mentor to the Shop Mentor in Weeks 2 and 3. Inside sales and customer service hires shift the other way.
