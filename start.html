Section 1: Getting Started

Who This Training Is For

This program is for every newcomer at Montgomery Rubber & Gasket: inside sales, counter sales, customer service, warehouse, and shop. Not everyone answers the phone, but everyone gets the same requests. A customer at the counter asks the same questions a buyer on the phone does.

By the end of three weeks you should be able to:

  • Find your way around Prophet 21 (P21) without help
  • Look up a customer, its ship-tos, contacts, credit, and buying history
  • Look up an item: stock, price, location, open orders, and where else in TRG it's stocked
  • Add and fix ship-tos and contacts
  • Enter a clean order, and build a quote when the customer needs a price first
  • Talk about our products and what our shop can build
  • Use the reference charts to size hose, identify a fitting, and pick a gasket

How to Use This Plan

Each day pairs one P21 skill with one product skill, then puts both to work with a mentor. Go section by section:

  1. Schedule: what's on today
  2. P21 Guides: step-by-step workflows to practice in P21PLAY
  3. Products & Capabilities: what we sell and what we make
  4. Product Samples: what each product looks like, where it's used, and example item IDs
  5. Reference: sizing charts, connection types, and quick lookups to keep handy
  6. Exercises: real-account practice tasks for each day
  7. Assessments: quizzes and the final exam

The Call & Counter Trainer is a practice tool. A customer makes a request; you pick the right questions and build a ticket until you know what to do in P21. Use it any time.

Ground Rules

  1. Wear safety glasses any time you cut hose or sheet, or operate shop equipment.
  2. The first half of each day is self-taught. Work the assigned guide in P21PLAY and the assigned reference sheet on your own. Write down questions for your mentor.
  3. In Weeks 1 and 2, every live order or quote you key gets a mentor review before it is saved or released.
  4. Ask early. A two-minute question beats a wrong part on a truck to a paper mill.
  5. Keep a training notebook. Write down every new term, part number family, and P21 tip.
  6. If you spot bad data in live P21 (a wrong address, a contact who left, a PO field with a name in it), write it down for your mentor. Don't change it yourself until you're signed off.

P21PLAY

P21PLAY is our P21 test system. It's a copy of live data. All practice orders, quotes, ship-tos, and contacts go in P21PLAY, never in live P21, until your mentor moves you to the live system.

Stock and order status in P21PLAY may differ a little from what the exercises show. When they differ, trust P21PLAY and write down what you found.

A note on field labels. The guides follow standard P21 labels. Our window layouts, security roles, and custom fields may differ slightly. On Day 1 your mentor walks each window with you; mark any differences in your notebook.

Daily Structure

Half Block Length Content
First half (self-taught) Block A 2.0 hrs P21 self-study: work the assigned guide step by step in P21PLAY
First half (self-taught) Block B 2.0 hrs Product self-study: Product Samples page, reference sheets, catalogs
Second half (with mentor) Block C 2.5 to 3.0 hrs Shadowing: phones, counter, warehouse, or shop
Second half (with mentor) Block D 0.5 to 1.0 hr Daily exercise, questions from your notebook, recap

Each Block D opens with your questions from the self-taught half. Your mentor checks the P21PLAY records you created that day.

Counter, warehouse, and shop hires: Block C shifts toward the counter, warehouse, and shop. Block A stays the same; everyone learns the same P21 basics.
